My Notes — Private, Encrypted Notes

Keep your thoughts safe and in sync across all your devices.

My Notes is a rich-text note-taking app built for people who care about privacy. Write, organise, and sync your notes across Android and macOS — with every note encrypted end-to-end before it ever leaves your device. No subscriptions, no ads, no data sharing. Just your notes, secured by your own Google Drive.

✍️ Rich Text Editor

Bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, 6 heading levels, ordered lists, bullet lists, checklists, and full undo/redo. Choose from system fonts or bundled decorative fonts (Nunito, Pacifico, Roboto Mono, and more). Set text colour, highlight colour, and font size.

🖌️ Format Painter

Copy formatting from one piece of text and apply it to another in one tap. Activate with the paint-brush icon in the toolbar.

📋 Rich Clipboard

Paste from Google Docs, web browsers, and other apps — bold, headings, bullet lists, and tables are preserved automatically.

🔗 Hyperlinks & Images

Attach URLs to any text. Insert images from your gallery or paste them directly from the clipboard. Long-press a link to edit or remove it.

✦ AI Writing Assistant

Tap the wand icon in the editor to generate an AI-powered rewrite of your note. Refine the suggestion with a follow-up prompt, then apply it in one tap. Supports Claude (Anthropic), Perplexity, Gemini (Google), and ChatGPT (OpenAI). Add your API key in Settings → AI Helper.

📄 Duplicate Note

Long-press any note and choose Duplicate to create an instant copy in the same folder. The duplicate does not inherit pinned state or sharing settings.

📌 Pin Notes

Pin important notes so they always appear at the top of every view. A sticky Pinned group keeps them visible across All Notes, folders, and inbox. Pin state syncs to Drive.

📁 Folders & Organisation

Organise notes into folders. Move notes with a long-press. Search across all notes by title and body content.

🌙 Theme Picker

Choose Light, Dark, or System in Settings → Appearance. Your preference is saved and restored on next launch.

🔗 Note Sharing

Share notes with other Google account users for real-time collaborative editing. Add collaborators by email, copy a web link, and manage access — all from within the app.

⚠️ Shared notes are stored in Firestore and are not end-to-end encrypted. Do not share sensitive information.

🔒 End-to-End Encryption

Every note is encrypted with AES-256-GCM before leaving your device. Your encryption key lives only in your Google Drive — never on a third-party server. No ads, no analytics, no data sharing.

⚠️ Exception: shared notes are stored unencrypted in Firestore. Do not share sensitive information.

☁️ Google Drive Sync

Changes sync across your devices within seconds using an incremental log. Only changed notes are downloaded, keeping bandwidth usage minimal. Works offline — notes are cached locally and sync when you reconnect.

👤 No Google Account Required

Sign in with a local username and password instead. Your encryption key is derived from your password and never written to disk.

🗑️ Account Deletion

Delete your account and all associated data (local + Drive) directly from the app. Cannot be undone.

💬 Send Feedback

Found a bug or have a suggestion? Use the in-app feedback form in Settings.
